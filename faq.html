FAQ

Questions, answered

 

Do I need to call or meet anyone?

No. Everything is done through the online form and email.

What does "complete info" mean?

All required fields in the form: business name, services, service area, hours, contact details and any photos or logo you want used. The 24-hour clock starts when everything is in.

What if I don't have photos or a logo?

We can use a clean text logo and suitable stock images. You can swap in your own later.

How do revisions work?

After you receive your first preview link, submit a Revision Request form. One form submission counts as one revision round. Two rounds are included in every package.

How long does each revision round take?

Each round is completed within 3 working days, and we email you a new preview link.

What can I change in a revision?

Included: text, images, colours, section order and fixing any information errors. Not included: changing template, adding new pages (from A$180 per page) or new features (quoted separately).

What if I need more than 2 rounds?

Extra rounds are A$99 each. Alternatively, the A$39/month care plan covers small edits every month.

How do payments work?

50% deposit before work starts, and the 50% balance after you approve the first version, before your site goes live. Add-ons are included in your total and split the same way. The optional care plan (A$39/month) is billed monthly after launch. GST not applicable.

How do I approve and go live?

Tick "Approve & launch" on the Revision Request form and pay the 50% balance. Your site then goes live. If we don't hear from you within 14 days of a preview link, the site is treated as approved.

Do I own my domain?

Yes. Your domain is registered in your business's name.

What add-ons are available?

Extra pages (A$180 per page), copywriting from your notes (A$160 per page), an English + Traditional Chinese version (A$350 setup plus A$60 per page for translation) and online booking setup (A$390). Tick them on the order form. See Pricing for details. GST not applicable.

Can you write the words for my website?

Yes, with the copywriting add-on. Send us your notes and we turn them into clear, professional copy (up to ~300 words per page). We only use facts you give us, and you approve the copy in your revision rounds.

Can my site be in two languages?

Yes: English and Traditional Chinese, with a language switch on every page. We don't offer other languages.

Can customers book online?

Yes. With the online booking add-on we embed your own Calendly or Cal.com booking page into your site. You need your own account and booking link; we don't set up or manage the booking account.

What's not included?

Online stores and e-commerce, member logins or booking systems built from scratch, custom-coded features, ongoing SEO or paid advertising campaigns, logo design, photography and guaranteed search rankings. Online booking is available only by embedding your own Calendly or Cal.com account.

How do I get help?

Send us a message via the online form or email ian.socialstudio@gmail.com.

Fill in one form. First version 24 hours after we receive your complete info.
