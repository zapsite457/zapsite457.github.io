## How a site is built

Three steps, all online. No calls, no meetings. A form and a preview link.

1. Fill in the form. Choose a template and describe the business: services, area, hours, photos and logo.
2. Zapsite builds. The first version is ready 24 hours after complete info is received. Zapsite emails a private preview link.
3. Review and launch. The customer can request up to 2 rounds of changes via the revision form. Once they approve, the site goes live.

- "Complete info" means all required fields: business name, services, service area, hours, contact details, and any photos or logo they want used. The 24-hour clock starts when everything is in.
- If they have no photos or logo, Zapsite can use a clean text logo and suitable stock images. They can swap in their own later.
- The order form is https://zapsite.com.au/get-started.html
- The form says fields marked * are required, and that Zapsite never invents reviews, qualifications or prices.
- Required on the order form: template, package, business name, ABN (11 digits), industry, contact name, email (only used to send the preview link and receipt), public phone, service area or address, opening hours, services or products, about us (3–5 sentences), desired domain (example shape mybiz.com.au), and agreement to the service terms.
- Optional on the order form: care plan, add-ons, logo file, photos (maximum 10), price list file, brand colour, customer reviews (only if the customer agreed to be quoted), a Facebook, Instagram or Google Business link, a booking or ordering link, language, and other notes (maximum 200 characters).
- Attachments (logo, photos and price list) must total 10 MB or less.
- Photos of a price list, menu or flyer are accepted, and Zapsite will type them up.
- Language field options on the form: English, Chinese, or English + Traditional Chinese (add-on).

- Included in a revision: text, images, colours, section order, and fixing information errors.
- Not included in a revision: changing the template, adding new pages (from A$180 per page), or new features (quoted separately). No separate price for new features is published.
- Extra rounds are A$99 each.
- Alternatively, the A$39/month care plan covers small edits every month.
- To go live, the customer ticks "Approve & launch" on the revision form and pays the balance.

- The revision form asks for the project or business name, email, and the round (1, 2, or extra at A$99). One submission should list every change wanted in that round.
